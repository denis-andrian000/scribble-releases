Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't signed yet. Click More info → Run anyway.

Scribble for Android

Scribble isn't in the Play Store. You install it straight from this page:

  1. Open this page on your phone or tablet and tap Download for Android.
  2. Open the downloaded file (Scribble-Android.apk). If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps, go back, and tap Install. If Play Protect warns about an unknown app, tap More details → Install anyway.
  3. Open Scribble, pick your language and tap Continue with Google. Use the same Google account as on your computer and your notes, tasks and chats appear.
  4. Floating buddy: tap the small window button at the top, or go to Settings → App → Floating buddy. The first time, Android asks to let Scribble display over other apps; turn it on for Scribble and come back.
  5. Tap the buddy to chat, hold it to open Scribble, drag it anywhere, or drop it on the ✕ to put it away.

Updates: Scribble tells you when a new version is ready (or check in Settings → App → Updates). Download it and tap Install; your notes stay as they are.

If the buddy disappears after a while, your phone may be closing Scribble to save battery. In Android settings, open Apps → Scribble → Battery and choose Unrestricted (the name varies by phone).
